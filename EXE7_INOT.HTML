Building the Future

By Kenneth Inot — BS in Information Technology (MCC)

Late nights glow with lines of code,

Tracing logic down the road.

Errors test the steady mind,

Yet solutions wait to find.

Every bug becomes a guide,

Turning doubt to quiet pride.

Step by step the skills expand,

Crafted by a patient hand.

Through the effort, through the climb,

Mastery is built in time.

Ready for what lies ahead,

Where bright ideas boldly tread.
